# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept feedback graciously; focus on the work, not the person.
- Be precise and honest about what you know and what you have verified.
- Respect the time of maintainers and other contributors.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behaviour, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict anyone whose behaviour they consider inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the **Security** tab as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (state that the report concerns conduct, not security) or through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers who are involved in an incident do not take part in deciding the response.

## Scope and attribution

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests and discussions) and when an individual represents the project in public spaces. It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, which is available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
