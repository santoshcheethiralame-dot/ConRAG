---
title: Library Extends Its Hours
source: Campus News Blog
date: 2025-03-20
---
The central library has announced extended hours for the exam season.

The library is open from 8 am to 10 pm on weekdays.

The library fine is Rs 5 per day for each overdue book.

Faculty members may borrow up to 10 books at a time for a period of 30 days.

A new silent reading hall has opened on the second floor.

Students can now reserve discussion rooms online.
