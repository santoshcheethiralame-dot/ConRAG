---
title: Hostel Rules
source: University Circular
date: 2025-07-01
---
The girls hostel gate closes at 10 pm every night.

The boys hostel gate closes at 11 pm every night.

Visitors are allowed in the hostel on Sundays from 4 pm to 6 pm.

Cooking is not permitted inside the hostel rooms.

Residents must register with the warden before leaving the campus overnight.
