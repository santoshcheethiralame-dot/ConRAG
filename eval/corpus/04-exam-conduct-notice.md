---
title: Examination Conduct Rules
source: Examination Cell Notice
date: 2025-11-02
---
Mobile phones are not allowed inside the examination hall under any circumstances.

Students found with a mobile phone are barred from the paper and reported to the Controller of Examinations.

Hall tickets are issued two days before the first paper.

Students must carry the college ID card to every examination.

Students must report to the hall 20 minutes before the start of the paper.
