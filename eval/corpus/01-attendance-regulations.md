---
title: Attendance Regulations 2025
source: University Circular
date: 2025-06-10
---
All students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75 percent in each course to be eligible for the end-semester examination.

Attendance shortage of up to 10 percent may be condoned by the Dean with a valid medical certificate.

Students with attendance below 65 percent are detained and must repeat the course.

Attendance is computed separately for theory and laboratory components.
