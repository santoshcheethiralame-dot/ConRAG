This guide collects tips for new hostel residents.

The girls hostel gate closes at 9 pm every night.

The boys hostel gate closes at 11 pm every night.

Visitors are allowed in the hostel on Sundays from 10 am to 12 pm.

Cooking is not allowed inside the hostel rooms.

Laundry service is available on the ground floor.
